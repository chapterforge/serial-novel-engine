===CHAPTER 1===
The Seat at the End of the Table

They had set my place at the end of the table, next to the kitchen door, where the servers came through with the soup.

"You're late," my sister said, without looking up.

"I was told seven."

"You were told six." She smiled at the man beside her. "She's always like this."

He looked at me for the first time. I knew the face from the newspapers, and I knew the ring on my sister's hand, because it had been my grandmother's, and it had been promised to me.

"Where did you get that?" I said.

Nobody answered. My father lifted his glass, and the room went quiet the way a room does when everyone already knows what is about to be said except you.

===CHAPTER 2===
Fifteen Days

I did not sleep. I sat on the floor of the old nursery with the box from under the bed open on my knees, reading the letter my grandmother had left, again and again, as if the words might rearrange themselves into something kinder.

They did not. She had written that the house would pass to whichever of us was married first. She had written a date. The date was fifteen days away, and my sister had been engaged for one evening, and I had been engaged for none.
